Your Child's Quran Journey Starts Here

Ubaid ur Rahman is a Hafiz-e-Quran (he has memorised the entire Quran) and a Fazil Graduate (a formal qualification in classical Islamic scholarship). He also holds expertise in Saba Qiraat — the seven authentic styles of Quranic recitation, a rare qualification held by very few teachers worldwide. He has years of experience teaching Quran reading, Tajweed and Islamic Studies. Lessons are calm, patient and structured — tailored to each student's pace, age and goals. For parents outside Pakistan, think of his qualifications like a recognised Islamic teacher's credentials: he has studied with qualified scholars and has formal training in teaching the Quran.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything international parents usually ask before booking a free trial.

What ages do you teach?

We teach children from around age 5 upwards, plus teenagers and adults. There is no upper age limit — we welcome adult beginners and everyone who wants to learn, regardless of age.

Do you teach adults?

Yes. A large portion of our students are adult professionals, university students, parents, and reverts who wish to learn Quran reading, correct their Tajweed, or study Islamic basics at their own schedule.

What is included in the free trial?

A live one-to-one session with the teacher where you meet, we assess the student's current level, run a short reading/Tajweed check, and give you a personalised learning recommendation. It's completely free with no obligation.

How do online classes work?

All classes are conducted live, one-on-one via Zoom or Google Meet. The student and teacher connect directly with interactive digital screen sharing of the Quran or Qaida lesson. Every lesson is interactive and tailored to the student's personal learning pace.

What Quran level can my child start from?

Any level — from complete beginners who have never seen Arabic letters to children who already read and want to improve Tajweed. We start from where your child is and always begin with a free assessment in the trial.

Do you teach Tajweed?

Yes. Proper pronunciation and Tajweed rules are central to our teaching. We offer both basic Tajweed (for those learning to recite) and advanced Tajweed for students who already read the Quran.

What are the class timings?

Classes are scheduled around your convenience. Because our students live in different time zones, we offer flexible scheduling including early mornings, afternoons, evenings and weekends to fit your family's routine.

Do you offer UK / USA / Canada friendly schedules?

Yes. Classes are scheduled in your local time zone, whether that's the UK, USA, Canada, or elsewhere. When you message us, simply let us know your country and preferred times and we'll arrange a slot that works.

How long is each class?

Classes are 30 to 45 minutes — short enough to keep young learners focused, long enough to make real progress. Depending on the plan you choose, students have 2, 3, or 5 classes per week.

How often are classes held?

Most families choose 2 or 3 classes per week for steady progress. Those pursuing intensive goals like Hifz (memorisation) often choose 5 classes per week. We'll help you pick the right rhythm.

Can girls and women join?

Yes. Sisters and girls of all ages are warmly welcome, in a completely respectful and professional one-to-one online learning environment. Parents are welcome to be present during lessons.

Can parents monitor progress?

Absolutely. We share regular progress updates with parents via WhatsApp, including areas of strength and what to practise next. You'll always know how your child is doing.

How does payment work?

Payment details are provided after enrollment by the teacher. We accept common international methods such as bank transfer, PayPal and Wise. Contact us for details relevant to your country.

What happens after the trial?

After the free trial, the teacher shares a personalised recommendation — the right course, class frequency and schedule for your child. You decide whether to continue, with no pressure and no obligation.

Can I book a trial for two siblings?

Yes — just let us know. We often teach multiple children in one family and can arrange assessments for both. Family and sibling discounts are available; message us on WhatsApp for details.
