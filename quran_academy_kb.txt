Q: What is Tajweed?
A: Tajweed literally means "beautification" or "improvement." In Quran recitation, Tajweed refers to the rules governing how each letter should be pronounced, including its articulation point (Makhraj), characteristics (Sifaat), and how letters interact. Proper Tajweed ensures you recite the Quran accurately. It is considered obligatory for every Muslim to recite with basic Tajweed.

Q: What is Saba Qiraat?
A: Saba Qiraat (also called the Seven Qira'at) are the seven authentic styles of Quranic recitation transmitted through unbroken chains from the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH). Each style has slight variations in pronunciation, vowelization, and recitation, but all convey the same divine message. Very few scholars worldwide are qualified to teach all seven, making this a rare and prestigious specialisation.

Q: What is Ijazah?
A: Ijazah is a certificate of authority and permission granted by a qualified scholar to a student, confirming that the student has mastered Quran recitation and can now teach the Quran to others. It includes a complete Sanad (chain of narration) going back to the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH). Our Ijazah program is for advanced students who have completed Hifz and Tajweed.
